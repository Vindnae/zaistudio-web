Dala and the Cursed Forest

Forge the cards... Unleash the spirit... Rewrite your fate...

Dala and the Cursed Forest is a dark fantasy roguelike deckbuilder. A maiden trapped in an endless cycle. Card Fusion reshapes her form mid-run, redefining your entire build. Every choice carries weight. Break the cycle before the curse consumes you.

Why You'll Love This Game

  • Fusion is Power: Don't just play cards — combine them! Merge your cards to create massive synergies and unlock much stronger versions. Finding the perfect combo is the key to victory.
  • Cool Transformations: As you collect "Cells", Dala can transform into different powerful forms. Each evolution unlocks new skills and completely changes how you fight mid-run.
  • Unique Fantasy Art Style: Step into a dark fantasy world inspired by Thai folklore and childhood tales. Experience a fresh, mystical aesthetic that stands out from any other Roguelike on the market.
  • Thai Cuisine & Lucky Shop: Visit the restaurant to power up with authentic Thai dishes for essential buffs. Feeling lucky? Try the Gacha box at the shop to grab powerful items and upgrades for your journey!

Dala and the Cursed Forest entered the depa Game Accelerator Program (Batch 2), where it achieved 1st place in the Strategy Game category. This victory was a major turning point, validating our vision and fueling our commitment to bring this unique Thai-inspired experience to the global stage.

Game Concept :

Master the Art of Card Fusion

Dala and the Cursed Forest is a dark fantasy roguelike deckbuilder built around transformation through fusion.

Combine cards to generate Cells, unlock new forms, and redefine your entire build mid-run.

Every fusion decision alters your strategy, shaping your playstyle, strengths, and risks in real time.

This is not just deckbuilding. This is evolution.

Transform Mid-Run

Gather matching Cells to awaken powerful forms, each granting new abilities, unique cards, and distinct combat styles.

Transformation is not a bonus. It reshapes your run.

Adapt your fusion path, experiment with build-defining synergies, and discover how each form changes the way you fight.

Strategic, Ever-Changing Runs — No two journeys through the cursed forest are alike. Choose from distinct starting decks. Discover new cards and items. Encounter mysterious events that bless... or twist your strategy. Every choice carries weight.

A Cursed Forest Inspired by Myth

Face mythical creatures and corrupted spirits drawn from dark folklore.

The deeper you venture, the more the forest pushes back.

Visit a feline-run restaurant and enjoy Thai-inspired dishes before your next battle.

Each meal grants powerful buffs that support your fusion strategy and prepare you for the challenges ahead.

Uncover the Truth Behind the Curse

As you progress, new story chapters and memories are unlocked. Piece together the fate of the cursed forest and discover the truth behind Dala's endless cycle.

Each run brings you closer to understanding the power that binds her.

What's Coming in the Full Release (Mid-2026)

We are working hard to make the game even better for our launch in the middle of 2026. Here is what you can look forward to:

  • More "Wow" Moments: We're upgrading the card fusion effects to look and feel more powerful than ever. Every combination will feel like a true evolution.
  • 4 Different Ways to Play — choose from 4 unique God Decks, each with its own playstyle:
    • The Fighter: A powerhouse deck that focuses on building a strong Block and Attack while melting enemies with Burn damage.
    • The Summoner: Mix cards to call out Magical Plants and ancient spirits to fight by your side.
    • The Debuff Spammer: Load your enemies with Curses and watch them explode as the corruption takes over!
    • The Magic Weaver: Create Special Zones on the battlefield that massively boosts the power of your magic spells.
