The Story Behind Dala

Told by Ussarin Maneewuttiworasakul (Nonae), Co-founder & Game Director

Dala began years ago as my graduation thesis: a 2D side-scrolling action game in the spirit of Odin Sphere. Even then, Dala collected flower petals to transform.

Transformation was the one thing that truly set the action version apart, so I rebuilt the game around it. Dala became a 2D side-view strategy game where fusing cards fills the Cells that power each transformation. An Asian art style inspired by Thai myth was also still rare in the genre.

Dala is made by a team of two. Artists who draw in this style are hard to find, so I designed and drew most of the game myself: the game design, the art direction, and most of the characters, scenes and animation.

“When making Dala, I wanted players to look at the cards in their hand and think, ‘What would I get if I fused these two?’ And then play that new card right away, in the same turn. For the art, I wanted the Himmapan forest to be so beautiful that you want to walk deeper into it, and for players to enjoy meeting its NPCs and monsters, each with a character all their own.”Ussarin Maneewuttiworasakul (Nonae), Co-founder & Game Director, Zai Studio

How we use AI

Dala’s characters, worlds and designs start with me: every idea, sketch and design decision is human. Finding artists who could work in Dala’s style was one of the hardest parts of the project, so I use AI as a tool to help with coding and rendering, so a very small team can finish a game of this scale. I don’t believe in AI art without human creativity behind it, and every asset in Dala is directed, drawn over and finished by hand.

Fuse Cards in the Heat of Battle

Combine cards mid-combat to create new effects. Every fusion also generates Cells that power Dala’s transformations.

Find Your Way to Fight

Choose from four starting decks, each with its own style:

  • Guwen: armor and fire
  • Tatara: plant allies
  • Vihok: chaining cards and poison
  • Vipak: water blasts and Naga hatchlings

Transform and Turn the Tide

Gather matching Cells to transform in the middle of battle. Each form brings its own abilities and cards.

Take a Break. Prepare Your Next Move.

A cat chef serves Thai-inspired dishes, such as mango sticky rice and durian, that help you recover or grant special effects. The shop sells cards and items for your next run.

Explore a Forest Inspired by Thai Mythology

Face mythical creatures and corrupted spirits, and meet events that can bless you or twist your strategy.
