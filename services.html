02

Game Production Consulting

Indie game production consulting for small teams: we help you cut scope, set milestones, and fix pipeline bottlenecks before time and budget go into work done in the wrong order. You keep your own team; we don’t take over production. Our advice comes from doing the work, not only planning it: Nonae has taken projects through every stage herself, from theory to hands-on practice.

IdeaPre-productionProductionTestingLaunchMarketingLive updates

How we help

  • Choosing the idea: find the concept with real potential, and scope it so your team can finish it.
  • Planning: people, time and budget, with clear milestones and priorities.
  • Systems and workflows: ways of working that keep the team efficient, using AI where it genuinely helps while human creativity stays at the core.

Who it is for

  • Teams who want to make a game but are unsure of the direction or the right process.
  • Teams already in development who need to decide what to add, what to cut, and how to reach the finish line.
  • Projects with a set budget and timeline that need a clear way of working.

Steam Key Art & Launch Assets

Game art and marketing assets for selected projects: Steam key art, capsule art and a matching set of visuals for your game launch, led by art direction rather than one-off images. We also take on in-game art. Art is our strongest skill: solid theory, hands-on experience and fast delivery.

  • Steam page and launch assets: key art, capsule art, banners and visuals for media and campaigns.
  • Character design: new characters and mascots that people remember.
  • Art direction: support from pre-production, to find the right style for your game.
