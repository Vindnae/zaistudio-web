Behind the Series

Told by Ussarin Maneewuttiworasakul (Nonae), Game Director

I wanted to make an 18+ game with characters people remember. Each of the seven demons is tied to one of the seven sins and serves a different fan’s taste, and every one of them has her own fan base today.

The armor-break idea came from other games in the genre, but I focused on what they lacked: beautiful art and smooth animation that feels good to play. Each demon reacts in her own way when hit, and after the fight players unlock rewards to keep the fun going.

I led the series as producer, game designer and art lead. The anime style made it easier to find artists to help on this series.

The 7 Sexy Sins series

Shoot Shoot My Waifu (7 Sexy Sins EP2) continues Raphael’s hunt in the human world, and 7 Sins : Lost in Labyrinth is a survival horror game set in the same world. It began as a short break project after a game competition, grew much bigger than planned, and turned out to be genuinely fun to play together.

Other versions: 7 Sexy Sins: Tadaima (original character-design version of Shoot Shoot My Waifu, on DLsite and Nutaku) and 7 Sexy Sins Mobile (Android).
