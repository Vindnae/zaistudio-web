About Zai Studio

An Independent Game Studio from Thailand

Zai Studio is the brand of ZAI STUDIO CO., LTD., an independent game studio based in Thailand.

We create our own games with distinctive art and gameplay, help game teams scope projects and plan production, and take on selected game art and marketing asset projects.

Our games include Dala and the Cursed Forest and the 7 Sexy Sins series.

Ussarin Maneewuttiworasakul (Nonae)

Co-founder & Game Director, Zai Studio

Ussarin Maneewuttiworasakul, also known as Nonae, co-founded Zai Studio after about 20 years working in game, animation and publishing studios.

She leads game design, art direction, character design, art and production, and also runs the business side of the studio herself: documents, accounting and HR.

On Dala and the Cursed Forest, made by a team of two, she is responsible for most of the game. Artists who draw in Dala’s style are hard to find, so she designed and drew most of the game herself, including most of the characters, scenes and animation, and reviews the game logic before the code structure is built.

Signature work

Every character in Zai Studio’s games is designed by Nonae.

The demon girls of 7 Sexy SinsFan favorites: each one embodies a different sin, has her own look and personality, and her own fan base.
  • Dala’s four gods and transformation forms: character design and animation.
  • Enemies with a story in their mechanics, like the Harpy lovers: their True Love skill means you have to defeat both at the same time, while the female pecks the male to raise her own Strength.
  • The card fusion system at the heart of Dala and the Cursed Forest.
  • The 7 Sexy Sins series: producer, game designer and art lead, with a small team.

Three rules for every Zai Studio game

  1. There has to be a cat.
  2. It has to be fun, with a unique selling point in its characters or its gameplay.
  3. The art has to be beautiful.

Making of Indie Game

Nonae’s book on the side of game development few people talk about, drawn from her own experience: what you need to know to make games, whether a game career is stable, the skills each role needs, funding and risk, expensive lessons, and how AI will change the games industry.

Written alongside her work with depa, and printed as a first edition of only three copies.
