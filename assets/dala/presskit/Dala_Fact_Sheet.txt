Short description
Fuse cards in your hand. Transform in the middle of battle. Command plant allies and unleash
powerful combos in a roguelike deckbuilder inspired by Thai mythology. The cards you combine
generate Cells that unlock new forms, each with unique abilities and cards.

Starting decks: Guwen (armor & fire), Tatara (plant allies), Vihok (chaining cards & poison),
Vipak (water & Naga hatchlings). All four are playable in Early Access.

Awards: 1st place, Strategy Game category, depa Game Accelerator Program (Batch 2)

The story behind Dala (told by Ussarin Maneewuttiworasakul, "Nonae")
Dala began years ago as my graduation thesis: a 2D side-scrolling action game in the spirit of
Odin Sphere, where Dala already collected flower petals to transform. Transformation was the one
thing that truly set it apart, so I rebuilt the game around it as a 2D side-view strategy game
where fusing cards powers each transformation. Dala is made by a team of two. Artists who draw in
this style are hard to find, so I designed and drew most of the game myself: the game design,
the art direction, and most of the characters, scenes and animation.

Quote
"When making Dala, I wanted players to look at the cards in their hand and think, 'What would I
get if I fused these two?' And then play that new card right away, in the same turn. For the
art, I wanted the Himmapan forest to be so beautiful that you want to walk deeper into it, and for
players to enjoy meeting its NPCs and monsters, each with a character all their own."
Ussarin Maneewuttiworasakul (Nonae), Co-founder & Game Director, Zai Studio

How we use AI
Dala's characters, worlds and designs start with me: every idea, sketch and design decision is
human. Finding artists who could work in Dala's style was one of the hardest parts of the project,
so I use AI as a tool to help with coding and rendering, so a very small team can finish a game of
this scale. I don't believe in AI art without human creativity behind it, and every asset in Dala
is directed, drawn over and finished by hand.
